Our Story

Three Generations of Craft, One Promise of Purity

Founded in 1978 by Late Shri Prakash Markase, Markase Jewellers began as a small workshop in Nagpur with a single principle: every piece must be exactly as pure as promised. Today, the same family runs the showroom, and that principle hasn't changed.

From bridal sets to everyday gold, each piece is hallmarked, certified, and backed by a straightforward exchange policy — because trust is what keeps three generations of families coming back.
